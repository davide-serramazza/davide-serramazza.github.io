Hey, I'm Davide Serramazza.
I am currently a PhD candidate in Computer Science at University College Dublin (UCD), conducting research on the application of Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI) techniques for Multivariate Time Series Classification.
My doctoral studies are fully funded by Science Foundation Ireland (SFI) through the ML-Labs research centre, and I am supervised by Dr. Georgiana Ifrim.
To enrich my research and foster international collaboration, I undertook a research visit to the Intelligent System Laboratory at the University of Bristol from April to July 2024, under the supervisorship of Dr. Zahraa Abdallah.
As I approach my graduation, I am exploring challenging opportunities where I can apply my research skills to complex problems. I am actively exploring my next career step and am particularly interested in roles that leverage my background in XAI to solve real-world problems, whether in an academic or industry setting.
My Background
My education was carried out at University of Pisa, Italy: my bachelor is in Computer Science, the final grade has been 100/110 while my master degree is Machine/Deep Learning having a final grade of 110/110.
Previous work experience is as researcher at University of Pisa, the project dealt with applying ML and Computer Vision techniques to process documentary films. The ultimate goal of the project was enhancing these documentaries, promoting their vision, trough automatic extractions of interesting short clips using ML/Computer Vision algorithms.
My research Projects In my PhD project I've been dealing with the following research questions:
  • Are specific algorithms for (M)TS needed or can we adapt generic explainers such as SHAP or LIME? In the second case, are any adaptations to (M)TS domain needed?
  • How do we objectively evaluate and compare multiple explanation methods for MTSC, especially for scenarios where their outputs disagree (e.g. for saliency-based methods, different explanations provide very different saliency maps)?
  • How can we integrate the insights gained from the explanation methods to improve the accuracy of the classification algorithms (e.g. an iterative optimisation approach)?
  • How can we integrate the insights gained from the explanation methods to improve or reduce the input data (eg, by removing noisy data, selecting good features)?
